Grammar Tips

Present Simple

Use it for habits and facts.

She reads every day. Water boils at 100°C.

Present Continuous

Use it for actions happening now.

I am studying English right now.

Past Simple

Use it for finished actions in the past.

We visited Chennai last year.

A vs. An

Use an before a vowel sound, a before a consonant sound.

an apple, an hour, a book, a university
